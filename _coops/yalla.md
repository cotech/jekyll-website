We are [Yalla](https://www.yallacooperative.com/), a cooperative of members across Palestine, the UK and the EU, united in our vision that digital solutions can drive positive, transformational change.  We partner with non-profits, NGOs, and impact-driven businesses to create innovative solutions through technology that address societal challenges, spread knowledge, and transcend borders.
